What it gates

PARTIAL
VERIFIED ONLY"Verified belayers only" sessions: joining checks your status for that category… but self-reported passes. The label promises verified; the check accepts a self-declaration.
TRADTrad sessions can require attestation — shows the badge on the session card; same self-report satisfies it.
Other climbers can't see your status. Verification lives in your phone's local storage — it isn't written to your Supabase profile, so real users browsing your profile see no belay status at all. (Demo climbers show theirs because theirs ships in the app bundle.)
To make it real: (1) write status to profiles so others can see it, (2) sync recap belay-confirms to a private table, (3) upgrade rule: e.g. 2+ distinct partners confirm → peer-confirmed, (4) tighten the verified-only gate to match its label.
